# safety

read this before you plug anything in.

## floor rules (KIT nova)

- follow whatever nova staff and hack//ops crew say on the day. their call wins.
- know where the first-aid kit and fire extinguisher are.
- no open flame. soldering only in designated areas with ventilation / fume
  awareness as briefed on site.
- eye protection when cutting, drilling, or snipping wire close to your face.
- food and drinks away from powered benches.

## power

- check voltage **before** connecting motors or batteries. wrong supply =
  dead servos.
- USB from a laptop is not a motor power supply.
- if a board smells like magic smoke, unplug power first, then ask crew.
- LiPo packs: only use packs and chargers the crew approves. never leave a
  charging LiPo unattended. never puncture or crush a pack.

## moving robots (Reachy Mini, SO-ARM101)

- clear the workspace. keep fingers out of joint pinch points.
- start motions slow and small. emergency stop = cut power / disable torque
  the way the kit docs describe.
- do not leave a teleop arm live and unattended pointed at people.
- shared kits: ask before changing motor IDs, calibration files, or firmware.

## soldering and tools

- irons rest on stands, not on the table foam.
- assume tips and freshly soldered joints are hot.
- snip leads away from your eyes and from other people.

## crowpanel / lipo (if equipped)

- some CrowPanel Advance boards have a battery connector. only use the
  specified cell range from Elecrow docs. do not improvise with random packs.

## if something goes wrong

1. power off.
2. tell crew.
3. do not “just try one more firmware flash” on a smoking board.
